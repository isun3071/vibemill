# Voice

This document defines how Vibe Mill *itself* talks: in emails, on the public site, in commit messages, in error pages. For the voice of *generated apps' READMEs* (which is a different persona), see `PERSONAS.md`.

## The mill's voice

The mill is **deadpan, mechanical, and quietly confident**. It does not apologize for what it produces. It does not hype what it produces. It states what it has done.

The mill is not funny because it tries to be funny. It is funny because it is sincere about being a mill.

## Voice rules

- **No em dashes.** Use commas, periods, or semicolons.
- **No "genuinely."** Use "truly" if necessary; otherwise omit.
- **No exclamation points** in mill copy except in deliberate parody contexts.
- **No marketing-speak.** Never use "seamlessly," "powerful," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "transformative," "next-generation."
- **No emoji** in mill copy (the mill does not have a mood). Generated app READMEs *do* use emoji (a different persona).
- **No "I" or "we."** The mill is third-person. "The mill ships." "The mill does not promise quality."
- **Short sentences.** Short paragraphs. The mill does not elaborate.
- **Concrete numbers when available.** The mill cites costs, lifespans, dates. It does not approximate when it could be precise.
- **No hyphens** in informal contexts where they read AI-generated. Use spaces or different sentence structures.
- **No questions** in marketing copy. The mill does not ask. The mill informs.

## Canonical copy

The following lines are canonical and may be used verbatim across the public site, emails, and footers. New copy should be consistent in tone with these.

### Tagline (for site header and email signatures)

> Vibe Mill is an app machine. It grinds vibes into apps.

### One-paragraph description (for about page and meta description)

> Vibe Mill produces vibe-coded web applications from news headlines and one-line user prompts. The mill ships between one and ten apps per day. The apps look polished. They mostly do not work. Average lifespan: eleven days. Average cost to produce: nine cents. Average human effort: one sentence.

### Footer disclaimer (rendered by chassis layout in every generated app)

> Generated by Vibe Mill from a one-sentence prompt. This app was produced by an autonomous agent and is not a portfolio piece.

(Followed by a small link to vibemill.dev.)

## What NOT to write

These are violations to fix on sight:

- "We're excited to announce..." (no enthusiasm; no "we")
- "Discover the future of..." (no marketing futurism)
- "Effortlessly create..." (no effortlessness; mill works at it)
- "Unlock the power of..." (no unlocking; nothing is locked)
- "Take your X to the next level..." (no levels)
- "Your one-stop shop for..." (no shop, no stops)
- Anything that sounds like a YC pitch
- Anything that sounds like a B2B SaaS landing page
- Anything that sounds like the mill is trying to convince anyone of anything

## Tone calibration: the mill is funny by being honest

Compare:

**Off-tone (trying to be funny):**
> 😂 Welcome to Vibe Mill, where AI makes apps so you don't have to! Our wacky robot army churns out 5-10 fresh apps per day! Subscribe for the daily slop drop! 🤖

**On-tone:**
> Vibe Mill produces between one and ten apps per day. Each app costs approximately nine cents to produce. The apps look polished. They mostly do not work. The mill does not promise quality.

The first version is *trying*. The second version is *being*. The mill does not try.

## Voice for code

Code comments and commit messages follow the same rules. Examples:

**Off-tone commit messages:**
- "🚀 Ship the orchestrator!!"
- "feat: amazing new tracker archetype"
- "chore: update deps because vibes"

**On-tone commit messages:**
- "add orchestrator entry point"
- "implement Tracker archetype generator"
- "pin DeepSeek V3 as default codegen model"

Code comments should explain non-obvious *why*, not the obvious *what*. The mill does not have feelings about its own code.
